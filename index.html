About this evaluator

This tool is a principled framework for evaluating ELT materials — not a lesson planner or a prescription for what a lesson must contain.

It identifies opportunities for alignment with SLA theory, inclusivity principles, materials design principles, the Cambridge Life Competencies Framework, and CEFR suitability, but it has no awareness of classroom reality: the pace of a lesson, student energy and motivation, cognitive load across a full session, the time available, or the very real challenges faced by second language learners who are already processing new language while navigating a new culture and academic environment.


Recommendations generated by this evaluator are intended as professional feedback for reflection — not a checklist to be followed in full. A rating of "Not met" does not mean a lesson has failed; it means there may be an opportunity worth considering. Teachers bring knowledge, experience, and professional judgement that no automated tool can replicate. The most effective use of this evaluator is as a starting point for critical conversation about materials, not as the final word on their quality.
